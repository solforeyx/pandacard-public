Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start trading.

What is Panda Card?

Panda Card is a next-generation digital card trading marketplace built by Solforeyx. Users can buy, sell, trade, and mine digital cards to build an online portfolio and earn rewards.

How does Card Mining work?

You can burn 3 eligible cards that you purchased from the Marketplace to earn 70% of their total value back as cash in your Earnings Balance. You can mine up to 2 sessions per day.

What are Diamond Points (DP) used for?

Diamond Points are earned by completing your Daily Combo streaks. They are a strict requirement for withdrawing your earnings to a bank account. You need 600 DP per ₦20,000 withdrawal.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum withdrawal is ₦20,000, or ₦5,000 if you are using a Bonus Card. There is a flat 15% fee on all withdrawals, which are processed manually by the Admin team.
